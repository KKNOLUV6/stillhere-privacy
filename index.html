Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

Still Here is built on one idea: your data stays yours.

What we collect

  • Journal entries, check-ins, and messages are stored only on this device.
  • Emergency contacts: the names, emails, and relationships you choose to add.
  • Location: only while “Walk Me Home” or SOS is active.

Location

  • Your location is used only during “Walk Me Home” or SOS, and only to share with the emergency contacts you selected.
  • The moment the feature ends, location sharing stops.

Notifications

  • We use notifications for daily check-ins and safety reminders.
  • You can turn them off anytime in Settings, or in your device’s system settings.

Storage & deletion

  • Everything stays on your device. Nothing is uploaded to a server.
  • Deleting the app removes all your data. There are no hidden copies.

What we don’t do

  • No ads. No tracking. We never sell or share your data with third parties.

Your rights

  • You can view, edit, or delete your data at any time, and withdraw any permission from your device’s settings.

Children

  • Still Here is not intended for children under 13.

Changes to this policy

  • If we update this policy, we’ll let you know inside the app.

Contact

  • Questions? Reach out at contact@beats-link-creators.com.
